# Changelog

## Unreleased

- portfolio-snapshot, market-analysis: a headline analysis card at the top of both pages, so the page says what stands out and what to look into first. The model writes it, and `render.py --summary FILE` holds it to 20 words for the headline, 90 for each paragraph and 70 for the folded "What this means for you", rejects the forbidden verbs, and lets links reach only that page's sections. Market Flows reads the accounts first, and leaves holdings off a page others can see or when there was no full read today. The chat reply gains the headline line, which replaces Market Flows' longer chat reading.
- portfolio-snapshot: 401(k) funds with no public quote now count toward the day change. Each borrows its day, 1w and 1m moves from the ETF tracking the same index (`day_change_proxy`), is marked as an estimate on the page and in `DAY_CHANGE_ESTIMATED`, and stays out of the movers. `PARTIAL_QUOTES` no longer fires when the uncovered share rounds to nothing.
- core-satellite: four weighting strategies, `equal` (the default), `market_cap`, `valuation` and `size`, set with `--weighting` or the saved `weighting`. Adding money (`add`, `grow`, plan steps, deposits, `new-pick` with `--amount`) only buys. `run.py rebalance` moves money between picks only on request, keeping the sleeve total and using no cash and no core sales: a pick is trimmed when above target by at least max(25%, $250), and any pick below target can receive the proceeds, in the same account. Picks due for a recheck hold their value (`HELD_FOR_RECHECK`, `DEEP_VALUATION_NEEDED`), a pick with no market cap holds under `market_cap` (`NO_MARKET_CAP`), and the skill re-checks due picks first. A new pick opens at the median pick, capped at 5%. The employer stock can be a pick when it has a saved deep valuation.
- core-satellite: a new pick opens in the trading account that holds the picks, not the one with the most cash, and `--sell SYM[,SYM]` names the core funds that pay for it (sold first, after cash). SPYM and other funds Yahoo labels a sector-less stock with a fund-shaped name count as core, not picks. A sweep fund the broker also reports as account cash (Fidelity's SPAXX) is counted once, not twice. With no employer ticker saved, the run reads two years of trading-account history for equity-plan vest deposits and returns `employer_candidate` (symbol, vest dates, cadence, days to sale) so the skill confirms the ticker instead of asking cold.
- portfolio-snapshot: the holdings table keeps Units, Price and Total in view. Long fund names and holdings spread over several accounts used to push the money columns off the right edge; those text cells now wrap, and the Value column reads Total.
- stock-screener: re-checks no longer wait for the 10-Q. `picks.py check --release FILE` rolls the TTM forward one quarter from the earnings release's GAAP income statement (flag `PRESS_RELEASE_FIGURES`); a recorded release check stays `awaiting_filing`, so the 10-Q check is still due. Saving a new deep valuation over a saved pick keeps its added date and price unless `--date` or `--added-price` is given.
- valuation: `inputs.py` also raises `CORPORATE_ACTION_SUSPECTED` when Yahoo's split record shows an adjustment after the latest annual statement. Yahoo restates share history for splits and spin-offs, so Honeywell's June 2026 spin-off passed the share-count ratio and its pre-spin cash flow was valued against today's company.
- portfolio-snapshot, stock-screener, valuation, trading: holdings are read before any change is discussed. A standing rule (`hooks/portfolio-check.md`, printed when a brokerage is connected) has the model work from a portfolio read taken today, never from the watchlist or pick store alone. A new UserPromptSubmit hook (`hooks/portfolio-freshness.sh`, `freshness.py`) adds one line with the local time and the age of the last read when it was on an earlier day. `snapshot.py` records every full read in `portfolio-last-check.json`.
- stock-screener: saved picks and re-checks. `picks.py` keeps each pick's baseline in the plugin data dir, so it outlives the session scratchpad: a deep pick's valuation (from its case folder, or recovered from a published deep page) and a light pick's fair values with its growth and margin at pick. `check` measures the latest reported quarters from EDGAR against each scenario's path (`track_math.py`) and says which scenario the company is tracking, whether it has left every scenario (the deep valuation is due again), and the return at today's price, without rerunning the valuation. Each check also places the price against the valuation (past the hurdle price, above the central value, above every scenario, below every scenario). A new SessionStart hook (`hooks/picks-summary.sh`) names picks due after earnings, a 15% price move or a crossing into a new price level. The deep picker saves its picks; deep valuation saves a baseline for held or tracked companies.
- trading, portfolio-analysis, connect, onboarding, setup: every confirmation ends the reply with a `### ⚠️ ACTION NEEDED` block that says nothing has happened yet and gives the exact reply for each order (or for the disconnect, profile delete or uninstall), with nothing after it. A yes asked mid-reply was missed in real use.
- stock-screener, valuation: deep-picker fixes. `edgar.py` throttles across processes and retries 429/503; `earnings.py` reads earnings releases from 8-K Item 2.02; `sentiment.py` reports FINRA short-sale volume and dated news; `inputs.py` takes the share count across classes, flags suspected corporate actions, large minority interests and cyclicals (with the latest-year base), weights debt net of cash in the WACC and uses a dated equity risk premium (`--erp`, `ERP_DEFAULT`, `ERP_STALE`); the screener flags large minority interests and corporate actions; deep pages and the picks page lead with the central case. The role briefs fetch from a dated primary-sources catalog before searching, the coordinator writes `context/macro.md`, finalists pass their industry notes into deep valuation, and the search plan keeps 40 in reserve (tasks 1-11).
- valuation, stock-screener: deep modes plan their web searches. Every role brief carries a hard search cap and fetches primary sources first; the coordinator keeps `budget.json` against the session's shared allowance, cuts thread rounds before finalists when it runs short, and reports searches used in the flags.
- stock-screener: deep picker mode. An industry scan and the financial screen run side by side, a lenient `health` preset (with `screen.py --symbols`) drops weak businesses, quick reads score each candidate on quality, direction, tailwind and value, and the finalists get a deep valuation; only those passing the downside gate (`gate.py`) are surfaced as picks on a new page (`render_pick.py`).
- valuation: deep mode. Research subagents read the filings and news, keep a sourced belief ledger, chase the contested beliefs, and value three to five named scenarios segment by segment (`model.py`), with the annualized return at today's price for each (`returns.py`) on a new page (`render_deep.py`).
- valuation: quick mode shows the bear, base and bull annualized return at today's price.
- valuation: deep mode reports what today's price implies, values banks, insurers and utilities on book value and ROE with matching charts and sensitivity axes, and its research follows blind-spot rules (structural-threat checklist, a source arguing the other side, a blind-spot thread each round).
